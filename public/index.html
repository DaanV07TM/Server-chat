Welcome to Server Chat

A quick guide before you start. Pick a channel, keep personal information private, and use the report flag if something is wrong.

  • Channels: #general for chat, #help for questions, #announcements for official updates, #voice for optional live voice, and #music for shared music playback.
  • Voice & screen sharing: Click Join voice before allowing microphone access. You can mute or leave at any time. Screen sharing is visible to room members and moderators may monitor or stop it.
  • Privacy: Text in private rooms is end-to-end encrypted in your browser with the invite-link key; public and official channel text is not end-to-end encrypted and can be read by the server. Images are not end-to-end encrypted and appear immediately in chat. Moderators can review images across public and private rooms, mark them reviewed, delete posts, and ban uploaders. Review is manual and cannot prevent harmful or illegal images from being seen before a moderator acts. This implementation is not independently audited, and a compromised server could serve modified browser code. Hosting providers may process connection metadata.
  • Safety: Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats, scams, or illegal content. Use ⚑ to report a message. Photos and GIFs are only available where enabled.
