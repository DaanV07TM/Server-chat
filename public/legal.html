Server Chat creates temporary accounts with generated credentials. Accounts, sessions and much of the service data are held in server memory and may disappear when the server restarts, redeploys or encounters a failure. There is no guaranteed uptime, backup, password recovery or permanent storage. Save your credentials securely; anyone who obtains them may be able to use your account.

Your content and moderation

You remain responsible for content you submit and must have the rights needed to share it. Public-room messages are processed as plaintext by the server. Content may be removed, and access may be limited, where reasonably needed for safety, moderation, maintenance or abuse prevention. The service cannot guarantee that harmful material will never be seen before moderation.

Private rooms and security limits

Private-room text is encrypted in the browser using a key carried by the private invite link. Anyone with that link may be able to read the room's messages, so only share it with intended participants. Images and other media are not covered by that text encryption. The application has not been independently security-audited; do not treat it as a guarantee of confidentiality or as a substitute for a professionally audited secure messenger.

If you use AI features, the prompts and conversation context you submit are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Music search uses the configured music service. The optional bot-check service may send verification data to Cloudflare Turnstile. These services have their own terms and privacy notices; do not submit sensitive information unless you are comfortable with the relevant processing.

Information handled by the application

  • Temporary account and session data: generated usernames, salted password hashes, session tokens and the date/version of the Terms accepted during registration. The raw generated password is returned once to your browser and is not retained as plaintext by the server.
  • Content and moderation data: messages, display names, reactions, reports, images, uploaded audio and other features you actively use. Public-room messages are visible to the server; images are not end-to-end encrypted and may be available to moderators.
  • Network and anti-abuse data: the application resolves a connecting IP address and keeps short-lived per-IP rate counters and temporary block entries in server memory to slow automated abuse and protect the service. Current application thresholds use 10-second request/event windows and a 60-second new-connection window; an automatic IP block lasts up to 10 minutes. The application does not intentionally write raw IP addresses to its chat history or application log.
  • Browser storage: the browser may keep a session token and preferences in local storage, and the service worker may cache the app shell. You can clear these in your browser settings.
  • AI prompts and external requests: when you choose to use AI, your submitted prompt and relevant AI conversation context are sent to the configured AI provider. Music searches are sent through the server to the configured music provider. If enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile processes bot-verification tokens.

Why the information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, authenticate sessions, maintain chat and moderation features, respond to reports, prevent account abuse and protect service availability. The operator must confirm the appropriate legal basis for each purpose. Operating the requested service may rely on steps needed to provide it; security and abuse-prevention processing may rely on a properly assessed legitimate interest where applicable.

Retention

Application account records, sessions and most chat data are held in memory, not a permanent application database. Chat history generally expires after about one hour; accounts, sessions and related in-memory data are normally cleared when the server process restarts or redeploys. Anti-abuse counters use short windows and temporary blocks expire after up to 10 minutes. These are application-level limits, not promises about hosting-provider or network logs.

Service providers and recipients

The hosting provider and network/proxy services necessarily handle connection traffic and may have their own operational logs under their own policies. Depending on which features are configured and used, information may be processed by Cloudflare Turnstile, an AI provider such as Groq, and the configured music catalogue provider. External providers may process data in other jurisdictions; check their current terms and privacy notices before using those features. The application does not intentionally sell personal information.
